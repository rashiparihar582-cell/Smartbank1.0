Stay Safe From Scams

Online banking scams are designed to steal your money, password, PIN, OTP, or personal information. Always stay alert before sharing any banking detail.

Never share: Password, transaction PIN, OTP, CVV, UPI PIN, card number, or bank account details with anyone. A real bank employee will never ask for these.

Common Scams

1. Phishing messages

Scammers may send fake emails or SMS messages that look like they are from a bank. They often create urgency, such as “your account will be blocked” or “verify your details now.”

Do not click unknown links. Always open the official website by typing its address yourself.

2. Fake customer care calls

A caller may claim to be from your bank and ask for an OTP, PIN, or card details to “stop a fraud” or “verify your account.”

Never share OTP, PIN, CVV, or UPI PIN on a call. Hang up and contact your bank using the official number from its website.

3. UPI QR code scam

To receive money, you only need to scan a QR code or share your UPI ID. You should never enter a UPI PIN to receive money.

If someone asks you to enter a UPI PIN to “receive” money, it is likely a scam.

4. Fake investment or lottery offers

Messages promising guaranteed returns, lottery winnings, cashback, or easy loans can be fraud attempts.

Do not pay an advance fee to receive a prize, loan, refund, or investment return.

Safe Banking Habits

  • Use a strong password and change it regularly.
  • Never save banking passwords or PINs in notes, messages, or email.
  • Do not use public Wi-Fi for banking transactions.
  • Check the website address before entering login details.
  • Review your transaction history regularly.
  • Report suspicious activity to your bank immediately.
Remember: If a message creates panic, promises easy money, or asks for OTP/PIN, stop and verify through official channels.
